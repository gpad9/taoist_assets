Title: The Stone Bridge Under the Water
Format: TikTok slideshow (7 slides)
Goal: Hidden support.
Style: Traditional Taoist Ink Wash (visual). Script voice: conversational fable—plain language, cause and effect, no lecture tone.

Slide 1 (Intro)
The Stone Bridge Under the Water.
(A stone bridge arch sits fully submerged in a swollen river, invisible except for ripples marking its ancient curve. Aged parchment background with soft ink washes.)

Slide 2
A village elder refuses to send carts across the flooded river, certain the old stone bridge has washed away in the night.
He trusts only what his eyes can see—but the bridge may remain, therefore the village stays divided.

Slide 3
The wise traveler (wise old Taoist sage with a long white beard and flowing robes) wades into the brown water and taps the surface with his staff until the stone arch answers with a solid, hollow knock.
The elder thinks the sage is foolish—but the bridge is there, therefore the crossing still exists.

Slide 4
The first cart rolls over the submerged arch, water parting around the wheels as the hidden stone holds firm beneath the flood.
What seemed destroyed was only covered—but the foundation endured, therefore the village reconnects.

Slide 5
Hidden support is not absence; it is the strength you forget is still there when the surface looks like total loss.
The Tao builds beneath the flood—but we judge by the waterline, therefore we abandon what still holds.

Slide 6
The elder marks the bridge on a new map with a note: invisible in flood, still present, and the carts cross until the river falls.
The water will recede—but the Tao is the arch, not the surface, therefore support remains.

Slide 7 (CTA)
Trust what holds beneath.
