Title: The Red Kite Tangled in the Tree
Format: TikTok slideshow (7 slides)
Goal: Patience.
Style: Traditional Taoist Ink Wash (visual). Script voice: conversational fable—plain language, cause and effect, no lecture tone.

Slide 1 (Intro)
The Red Kite Tangled in the Tree.
(A bright red paper kite hangs tangled in the bare branches of a winter tree, its string wound tight around the trunk. Aged parchment background with soft ink washes.)

Slide 2
A child yanks the kite string hard, trying to free the red paper from the bare branches before his friends see it stuck.
He wants instant rescue—but the string tightens, therefore the kite tears.

Slide 3
The wise traveler (wise old Taoist sage with a long white beard and flowing robes) takes the string gently and walks it backward, loosening each loop around the trunk with patient fingers.
The child thinks slow work is failure—but the knots release, therefore patience untangles what force worsens.

Slide 4
The kite slides free from the highest branch and catches a soft breeze, rising again over the grey winter field.
It was never lost—but the child almost destroyed it pulling, therefore gentleness saved the flight.

Slide 5
Patience is not waiting without action; it is working with the knot instead of fighting the string that holds what you love.
The Tao frees by loosening—but we pull harder, therefore the kite rips.

Slide 6
The child runs with the kite and lets the sage hold the spool, learning to give slack when the wind asks for room.
The tree is empty now—but the Tao is the loosening, not the yank, therefore the kite flies.
