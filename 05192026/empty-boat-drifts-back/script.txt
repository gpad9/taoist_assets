Title: The Empty Boat Drifts Back
Format: TikTok slideshow (7 slides)
Goal: Non-reaction.
Style: Traditional Taoist Ink Wash (visual). Script voice: conversational fable—plain language, cause and effect, no lecture tone.

Slide 1 (Intro)
The Empty Boat Drifts Back.
(An empty wooden boat drifts back toward shore on the current, bumping softly against the dock with no one aboard. Aged parchment background with soft ink washes.)

Slide 2
A fisherman shouts at an empty boat that bumped his dock, accusing the vessel of disrespect and carelessness.
He treats the boat like an enemy—but no one is aboard, therefore his anger has no target.

Slide 3
The wise traveler (wise old Taoist sage with a long white beard and flowing robes) catches the boat's rope and ties it gently, explaining that an empty boat cannot intend harm.
The fisherman thinks this is nonsense—but the boat is empty, therefore the offense was his projection.

Slide 4
The fisherman sits on the dock and watches other boats drift by, some touching his post, none carrying malice because none carry a grudge.
The river moves everything—but he was fighting ghosts, therefore his peace returns.

Slide 5
Non-reaction begins when you stop treating empty boats like enemies and see that most of what offends you carries no intention at all.
The Tao floats without argument—but we shout at the drift, therefore we drown in invented battles.

Slide 6
The fisherman releases the rope and lets the empty boat drift on, his voice finally as quiet as the water.
The dock is still—but the Tao is the empty boat, not the shout, therefore peace remains.

Slide 7 (CTA)
Let the boat drift.
