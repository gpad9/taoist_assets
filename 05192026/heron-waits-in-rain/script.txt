Title: The Heron Waits in the Rain
Format: TikTok slideshow (7 slides)
Goal: Stillness.
Style: Traditional Taoist Ink Wash (visual). Script voice: conversational fable—plain language, cause and effect, no lecture tone.

Slide 1 (Intro)
The Heron Waits in the Rain.
(A grey heron stands motionless in shallow rain-pitted water, wings folded, eyes fixed on the rippling surface. Aged parchment background with soft ink washes.)

Slide 2
An anxious fisher casts his line again and again in the rain, moving every few breaths because stillness feels like failure.
He believes action catches fish—but the water stays empty, therefore his restlessness scares the pool.

Slide 3
The wise traveler (wise old Taoist sage with a long white beard and flowing robes) points to a grey heron standing perfectly still in the same rain, not moving even when drops strike its head.
The fisher thinks the bird is lazy—but the heron waits, therefore the fish forget to fear.

Slide 4
A silver fish finally breaks the surface near the heron's beak, caught not by frenzy but by the patience of a body that did not flinch.
The fisher cast a hundred times—but the heron moved once, therefore stillness outfished effort.

Slide 5
Stillness is not the absence of hunger; it is the discipline to remain whole and quiet until the moment arrives on its own terms.
The Tao hunts without hurry—but we thrash the water, therefore the fish flee.

Slide 6
The fisher sets his rod down and stands like the heron until the rain softens and his own breathing matches the ripples.
The marsh is grey—but the Tao is the waiting, not the cast, therefore stillness remains.
